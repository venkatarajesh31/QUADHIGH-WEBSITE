Our engagement models

Every NetSuite project is different, so we offer two flexible ways to work with us. Both are based on the hours your project needs.

Time & Materials

Best for projects where requirements may evolve.

  • Pay for the hours actually worked.
  • Get an estimate up front and regular updates on hours used.
  • Adjust priorities as the project evolves.

Fixed-Fee

Best for well-defined projects with a clear scope.

  • We estimate the total hours up front and agree on one price.
  • You know the cost before we start.
  • We discuss and get your approval before starting work outside the agreed scope.

Both models are available for all our services.

Our typical implementation process

Six steps from understanding your business to supporting your team after go-live.

  1. 1

    Discover

    Understand your workflows, reporting needs and priorities. Agree on the scope together.

  2. 2

    Design

    Map your processes to NetSuite and agree on configuration, access and integrations.

  3. 3

    Build

    Configure NetSuite, develop extensions and prepare your data for migration.

  4. 4

    Test

    Run everyday scenarios with your users, verify data and address issues before launch.

  5. 5

    Go live

    Coordinate the final data transfer, prepare users and move to the live system.

  6. 6

    Support

    Resolve early issues, answer user questions and agree on what to improve next.

AI Advantage

We use AI-assisted tools to speed up selected development tasks. Our NetSuite team supervises their use, reviews the results and stays accountable for the quality of the work.

Rapid Development Cycles

AI can help with repetitive work such as drafting standard SuiteScript, preparing data transformation scripts and outlining regression tests. That gives our team more time for the work that needs NetSuite expertise.

Multi-Layer Human Inspection

Our NetSuite architects review code and configuration for business fit, access controls and SuiteCloud limits. The team checks the result in the context of your system before approving it.

Enterprise-Grade Reliability

We test changes against real workflows, including the integrations and data they affect. Before release, we consider how the changes will be maintained and what they could mean for future NetSuite updates.

A Method That Fits the Project

We decide where AI makes sense based on the task, the sensitivity of the data and whether we can verify the result. Some tasks are better handled through direct analysis or hands-on configuration.
