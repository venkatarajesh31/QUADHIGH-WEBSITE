A few useful answers

Before we talk.

We can start with a new implementation or a specific issue you want to solve.

Can you help with an existing NetSuite account?

Yes. Our services cover existing accounts as well as new implementations, including configuration improvements, custom development, integrations and managed support.

Can you work alongside our existing NetSuite partner?

Yes. Our advisory and oversight offering provides client-side project management and solution reviews. We agree responsibilities and how to collaborate at the start of the engagement.

Do we need a full implementation project?

No. You can discuss a specific enhancement, integration, storefront requirement or support need. We’ll scope the work around the problem you want to solve.

How do you estimate cost and timelines?

We first understand your requirements, current setup, data and integrations. We then discuss an estimate and an appropriate engagement model, including any dependencies on your team.
